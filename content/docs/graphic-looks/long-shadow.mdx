## Usage

`LongShadow` is a full-frame scene. The icon springs in from nothing at the
center of the frame. Its shadow grows out of it until it reaches the frame
edge, a solid band the exact shape of the icon's silhouette. Then the light
swings, and the shadow sweeps from `angleFrom` to `angleTo` on a soft spring
that overshoots a little before it settles.

```tsx
import { LongShadow } from "@/components/remocn/long-shadow";

export const Done = () => <LongShadow />;
```

Without children it shows a red check badge. Pass a flat icon or mark as
children to cast its shadow instead. The children are centered, and the
shadow is built from their alpha, so any opaque shape works: an SVG, an image
with transparency or plain text.

```tsx
import { LongShadow } from "@/components/remocn/long-shadow";

export const AppIcon = () => (
  <LongShadow
    angleFrom={30}
    angleTo={150}
    shadowColor="#1F4FD1"
    background="#2D6BE5"
    fade={0.6}
  >
    <div
      style={{
        width: 240,
        height: 240,
        borderRadius: 56,
        background: "#FFFFFF",
      }}
    />
  </LongShadow>
);
```

Angles are in degrees, clockwise from the right: `0` casts the shadow to the
right and `90` casts it down. `fade` fades the shadow toward its far end,
from `0` for a solid band to `1` for a shadow that disappears at the edge.

## Timing

At 30 fps and `speed={1}`:

| Frames | Motion |
| --- | --- |
| 0–19 | The icon and its shadow spring in from zero scale |
| 7–22 | The shadow grows out to the frame edge at `angleFrom` |
| 24–48 | The light swings and the shadow sweeps to `angleTo`, overshooting slightly |

`longShadowLength` is 48, the frame the swing settles. `speed` scales the whole
timeline.

## Notes

The shadow is an SVG filter that stacks 1023 offset copies of the silhouette
in ten doubling passes, then softens the steps with a hair of blur. It covers
the full frame, so expect slower renders than a plain scene, especially at 4K.
