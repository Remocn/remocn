## Usage

`Synthwave` is a full-frame background. A perspective grid glows on the floor
and scrolls toward the viewer. A striped sun rises from behind the horizon,
its stripes thickening toward the bottom and drifting downward. Two mountain
ranges slide in from opposite sides as they rise into place, the front one
traced in the grid color. Stars twinkle in the dark sky, and a haze swallows
the lines where they converge.

```tsx
import { AbsoluteFill } from "remotion";
import { Synthwave } from "@/components/remocn/synthwave";

export const Drop = () => (
  <AbsoluteFill>
    <Synthwave grid="#00E5FF" sunTop="#FFE66D" sunBottom="#FF2E88" />
    <AbsoluteFill style={{ alignItems: "center", paddingTop: 120 }}>
      <Title />
    </AbsoluteFill>
  </AbsoluteFill>
);
```

It takes no children. Layer your content on top in a sibling `AbsoluteFill`,
and keep it above the horizon, which sits at `horizon` of the frame height
(60% by default).

## Timing

At 30 fps and `speed={1}`:

| Frames | Motion |
| --- | --- |
| 0–40 | The sun rises from behind the horizon |
| 4–34 | The back range slides in from the left and rises |
| 8–38 | The front range slides in from the right and rises |
| Always | The grid scrolls at `gridSpeed` rows per second; the sun stripes cycle every 36 frames |

`synthwaveLength` is 40, the frame the sun and mountains are in place. The
grid and stripes never stop, so the background holds for any duration.
`speed` scales the entrance and the scroll together.
