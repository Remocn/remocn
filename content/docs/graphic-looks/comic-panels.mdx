## Usage

`ComicPanels` is a full-frame scene. Your content starts centered on the page,
surrounded by ink speed lines that are redrawn every two frames. A page border
closes in, and a slanted gutter cracks across the frame and lifts the content
into the top panel. A second gutter cracks down, and two panels slide in
underneath: a halftone panel in the accent color from the left and an ink
panel with a small starburst from the right. The page shakes, and the
starburst grows until its accent rays fill the frame, leaving a paper burst
with the word in the middle.

```tsx
import { ComicPanels } from "@/components/remocn/comic-panels";

export const Launch = () => (
  <ComicPanels word="POW">
    <Logo />
  </ComicPanels>
);
```

`children` is the hero content. It is rendered at full size at first and
scales to `heroScale` as it moves into the top panel, so size it as you would
for a full frame. Without children a placeholder "remocn" lockup is shown. `word` is optional. Without it the starbursts are empty. Keep
the word short: it is set large and bold, and three or four letters read best.

## Timing

At 30 fps and `speed={1}`:

| Frames | Motion |
| --- | --- |
| 0–8 | The page margin and ink border close in |
| 2–10 | The horizontal gutter cracks across; the hero rises and scales to `heroScale` |
| 8–14 | The vertical gutter cracks down to the bottom edge |
| 10–18 | The halftone panel slides in from the left |
| 13–21 | The ink panel with the starburst slides in from the right |
| 22–31 | The page shakes |
| 23–34 | The starburst grows and spins until the accent fills the frame |
| 30–36 | The paper burst settles from a slight overshoot |

`comicPanelsLength` is 36. The final burst keeps a gentle 4% pulse after that,
so the hold never freezes. `speed` scales the whole timeline.
