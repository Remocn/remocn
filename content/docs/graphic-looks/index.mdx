---
title: Graphic Looks
description: Full-frame scenes that borrow a print, drawing or screen technique, from risograph plates and blueprints to heat cameras and toy bricks
---

Each look is a complete, full-frame scene with its own ground. It plays a short entrance, then holds the finished image, or keeps it alive with a boil, a beat or a scrolling floor. Most take your own subject as children and fall back to a built-in demo subject without them.
