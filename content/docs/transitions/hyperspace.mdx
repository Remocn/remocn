## Usage

`hyperspace()` is a Remotion transition presentation. Pass it to a
`TransitionSeries.Transition` between two sequences. A starfield fades in over
the outgoing scene, which dims and pushes forward. The stars drift slowly, then
surge outward from the center as their streaks stretch with speed, and the
frame ramps to `flashColor` at 62% of the transition. The incoming scene lands
under the flash: the flash holds for a beat and dissolves while the scene eases
out of a slight zoom and blur, with a few streaks still coasting past.

The star layout is deterministic. Each scene renders once; the stars are one SVG
with up to `stars` lines, and the arrival adds a CSS blur for its first frames.

```tsx
import { TransitionSeries, linearTiming } from "@remotion/transitions";
import { hyperspace } from "@/components/remocn/hyperspace";

export const MyVideo = () => (
  <TransitionSeries>
    <TransitionSeries.Sequence durationInFrames={90}>
      <SceneA />
    </TransitionSeries.Sequence>
    <TransitionSeries.Transition
      timing={linearTiming({ durationInFrames: 56 })}
      presentation={hyperspace()}
    />
    <TransitionSeries.Sequence durationInFrames={90}>
      <SceneB />
    </TransitionSeries.Sequence>
  </TransitionSeries>
);
```

## Timing

| Progress | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 0.62 | Stars fade in and spool up, the scene dims and pushes forward |
| 0.45 to 0.62 | Flash ramps up to full |
| 0.62 | Cut to the incoming scene under the flash |
| 0.62 to 1 | Flash dissolves, the scene settles out of zoom and blur |

## Variations

- `flash: false` swaps the white-out for a hard cut through the streaks at 62%.
- `accentRatio: 0` keeps every streak in `streakColor`; raise it to tint more stars with `accentColor`.
- Fewer `stars` (80 to 120) gives a sparser, calmer jump.
