---
title: Camera & Physics
description: Full-frame shots built on a simulated camera or a small physics engine, from parallax trucks and a dolly zoom to falling bodies and a cloth yank
---

Each piece here owns the whole frame. The camera shots project a small 3D scene through a pinhole camera, and the physics pieces run a deterministic simulation once and play it back, so seeking and rendering always land on the same frame. Most ship with a built-in subject and accept your own content as children or layers.
