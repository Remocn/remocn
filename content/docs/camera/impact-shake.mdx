## Usage

`ImpactShake` drops its subject from above the frame on pure acceleration,
stretching it taller as it falls. On the impact frame the subject squashes
wide and springs back, the whole camera jolts and settles on a damped shake,
red and blue copies split apart for two frames, the frame flashes faintly, and
dust puffs and small chips kick out sideways along the floor line. With no
children it drops the word in `text`.

```tsx
import { ImpactShake } from "@/components/remocn/impact-shake";

export const Drop = () => <ImpactShake text="NOW LIVE" intensity={1.2} />;
```

`intensity` scales the shake, the split, the flash and the dust spread, from
0 to 2; the squash stops growing above 1. `impact` is the frame, at 30 fps,
when the subject hits, so a larger value makes a longer fall. `seed` changes
the shake phase and the dust pattern.

### Your own subject

Children replace the word. They are centered in the frame, and the dust
kicks out from a floor line at 62% of the frame height, which lines up with
the bottom of the default word. Size the subject so its base sits near that
line.

```tsx
import { Img, staticFile } from "remotion";
import { ImpactShake } from "@/components/remocn/impact-shake";

export const LogoDrop = () => (
  <ImpactShake backgroundColor="#0B0B0B">
    <Img src={staticFile("logo.svg")} style={{ height: 180 }} />
  </ImpactShake>
);
```

During the two split frames the subject renders three times, the two extra
copies tinted through an SVG color filter. `colors.splitA` and
`colors.splitB` must be `#RRGGBB` hex. The flash uses the foreground color.

## Timing

At 30 fps, `speed={1}` and the default `impact` of 7:

| Frames | Motion |
| --- | --- |
| 0–7 | The subject falls in from above, accelerating and stretching |
| 7 | Impact: squash, camera jolt, flash and dust burst |
| 7–9 | Red and blue copies split apart and close |
| 7–20 | The camera shake decays to nothing |
| 7–30 | Dust hangs and fades, and the squash wobble settles |

`speed` scales the timeline, so `speed={2}` lands at about frame 4.
