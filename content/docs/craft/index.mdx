_Last updated: November 19, 2026_

Remotion videos made by Claude Code, Cursor or Codex look generic when the agent falls back on its
defaults: glow behind every heading, gradient text, ALL-CAPS, and every panel popping in on the same
frame. The Craft pages are the rules that replace those defaults. The
[Remocn agent skill](/docs/getting-started/agent-skill) points your agent here, and you can paste
the same rules into any prompt.

The component pages tell you what each block does. This section tells you how to put them together
without the result reading as machine-made.

## The eight mistakes that make an AI video look generic

| # | Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Glow halos and heavy shadows** behind headings and cards | No colored glows, no blur over ~24px. A solid fill or a slow, muted shader. [Rule](/docs/craft/design-defaults#no-glow-or-heavy-shadows) |
| 2 | **Gradient-filled text** standing in for emphasis | Solid text. Mark the one word that matters with weight, the accent color or a highlight. [Rule](/docs/craft/design-defaults#no-gradient-text-fills-or-decorative-washes) |
| 3 | **ALL-CAPS with wide letter-spacing** on text the agent adds | Sentence case, default tracking. [Rule](/docs/craft/design-defaults#no-decorative-letter-spacing) |
| 4 | **Everything enters on one frame** | Stagger sibling entrances by 3–6 frames, one focal action per beat. [Rule](/docs/craft/anti-patterns#everything-enters-on-one-frame) |
| 5 | **Animations cut off mid-motion** because the `Sequence` is shorter than the component | Budget each `Sequence` at least the component's natural length, listed in [`/llms-components.txt`](https://remocn.dev/llms-components.txt). [Rule](/docs/craft/anti-patterns#clipping-a-component-by-under-budgeting-its-sequence) |
| 6 | **A transition mounted as a component** | Pass it to `TransitionSeries.Transition` as a presentation. [Rule](/docs/craft/anti-patterns#mounting-a-transition-as-a-component) |
| 7 | **Animating `top`, `left`, `width` or `height`** | Animate `transform`, `translate` and `scale` only. [Rule](/docs/craft/anti-patterns#animating-layout-properties) |
| 8 | **`Math.random()`, `Date.now()` or timers in render code**, which flicker between frames | Seeded `random()` from `@remotion/random`, everything derived from the frame. [Rule](/docs/craft/anti-patterns#non-deterministic-code) |

Remocn's own components are exempt where the effect *is* the point:
[`tracking-in`](/docs/typography/tracking-in) animates letter-spacing on purpose, and the shader
backgrounds are gradients by design. The rules apply to what your agent writes by hand.

## The three Craft pages

Three pages, in the order they bite:

- [Design defaults](/docs/craft/design-defaults) — what your **own** additions should look like.
  Sentence case, default tracking, solid text, no glow, and a fixed palette and font set. The library's
  components are exempt where the effect *is* the point.
- [Motion principles](/docs/craft/motion-principles) — the classic animation principles reduced to
  the eight that matter when you assemble scenes from prebuilt blocks, each mapped to a Remotion
  mechanic and a component that demonstrates it.
- [Anti-patterns](/docs/craft/anti-patterns) — the full list of a dozen mistakes that recur, with
  the fix beside each one. Beyond the eight above: animation props on a UI primitive, the wrong
  canvas size, loading fonts mid-render, and promoting the wrong layer under a slow zoom.
