## Usage

`Inflate` draws its own mark: a glossy rounded square with a dot in the
middle. It starts as a flat, deflated sliver, then puffs up on an underdamped
spring, overshoots, squashes wide and rings out like a balloon. As it fills,
the corners tighten from a pill to a rounded square and a specular highlight
and inner shadow fade in. The dot pops in last, then the mark slides left
while your wordmark wipes in on its right.

```tsx
import { Inflate } from "@/components/remocn/inflate";

export const LogoSting = () => (
  <Inflate color="#E5322D" dotColor="#111111">
    <span
      style={{
        fontSize: 104,
        fontWeight: 800,
        letterSpacing: "-0.04em",
        color: "#FFFFFF",
      }}
    >
      remocn
    </span>
  </Inflate>
);
```

Pass only the wordmark as `children`. Without `children` the mark stays
centered and no slide happens. The mark is 22% of the shorter side of the
composition (158px at 720p); the wordmark keeps its own size.

## Timing

At 30 fps and `speed={1}`:

| Frames | Motion |
| --- | --- |
| 0–3 | A flat sliver fades in and widens |
| 3–26 | The body puffs up on a spring, peaking about 23% oversize around frame 10 |
| 10–30 | Squash-and-stretch wobble that decays to rest |
| 5–12 | Highlight and inner shadow fade in |
| 15–32 | The dot springs in with a small overshoot |
| 22–33 | The mark slides from the center to the left |
| 24–35 | The wordmark wipes in from the left |

The docs preview runs 66 frames. `speed` scales the timeline.
