---
title: Logo Stings
description: Short brand reveals that land your logo or wordmark at the open or close of a video
---

import { CategoryIndex } from "@/components/docs/index/category/category-index";

A logo sting is the one or two second beat where the brand lands. Each one fills the frame, plays its own lead-in, and leaves your lockup centered and holding, so it works as the first or last `Sequence` of a video. Most take the logo as children; a few draw a mark themselves and only need the wordmark.
