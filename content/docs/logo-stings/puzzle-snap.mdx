## Usage

`PuzzleSnap` draws its own mark: a quarter circle, a dot, a wedge and a small
rounded square on a 2×2 grid. Each piece starts far off at a seeded angle,
drifts in while it spins, then gets yanked onto its slot over three frames and
throws a ring of six sparks. When the last piece lands the whole mark punches,
then slides left while your wordmark wipes in on its right.

```tsx
import { PuzzleSnap } from "@/components/remocn/puzzle-snap";

export const LogoSting = () => (
  <PuzzleSnap primaryColor="#E5322D" secondaryColor="#FFFFFF">
    <span
      style={{
        fontSize: 104,
        fontWeight: 800,
        letterSpacing: "-0.04em",
        color: "#FFFFFF",
      }}
    >
      remocn
    </span>
  </PuzzleSnap>
);
```

Pass only the wordmark as `children`, not a full lockup: the mark is the
sting's own drawing. Without `children` the mark stays centered and no slide
happens. The mark is 20% of the shorter side of the composition (144px at
720p), and the wordmark keeps its own size. Positions come from Remotion's
seeded `random()`, so the same `seed` always renders the same frames.

## Timing

At 30 fps, `speed={1}` and `square` on:

| Frames | Motion |
| --- | --- |
| 0–9 | Pieces launch one every 3 frames and fade in over 3 frames |
| 0–22 | Each piece drifts in for 10 frames with an ease-out, then snaps the last stretch in 3 frames |
| 13, 16, 19, 22 | Contact frames; each throws a spark ring that lasts 8 frames |
| 22–38 | The mark punches from 108% and springs back to full size |
| 24–35 | The mark slides from the center to the left |
| 26–37 | The wordmark wipes in from the left |

With `square={false}` there are three pieces and everything after the last
contact starts 3 frames earlier. The docs preview runs 66 frames. `speed`
scales the timeline.
