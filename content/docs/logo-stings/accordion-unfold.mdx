## Usage

`AccordionUnfold` prints your logo across a paper card, then folds the card
into vertical pleats. It starts as a tight zigzag and opens sideways on a
smooth spring with no overshoot, staying centered as it widens. Pleats facing
away from the light are shaded and the ones facing it catch a faint
highlight, so the folds read in 3D. When the card is flat it switches to a
single plain card, so no seams are left in the hold.

```tsx
import { Img, staticFile } from "remotion";
import { AccordionUnfold } from "@/components/remocn/accordion-unfold";

export const LogoSting = () => (
  <AccordionUnfold color="#FFFFFF" panels={6}>
    <Img src={staticFile("logo-dark.svg")} style={{ height: 96 }} />
  </AccordionUnfold>
);
```

`children` are centered on the card, and each pleat shows its own slice of
them, so the logo folds with the paper. Use a version of your logo that reads
on `color`: the default card is white, so a dark logo works best. The card is
66% of the frame width, capped at 130% of the height, and 40% of the frame
height tall, capped at 40% of its width. Children larger than the card are
cropped at its edges.

The fold uses CSS 3D transforms (`rotateY` with `preserve-3d`), which Remotion
renders in Chrome as expected.

## Timing

At 30 fps and `speed={1}`:

| Frames | Motion |
| --- | --- |
| 0–3 | The folded card fades in |
| 0–6 | The card lifts from 92% to full scale |
| 4–26 | The pleats open on a spring and the card lies flat |

The docs preview runs 66 frames to hold the flat card. `speed` scales the
timeline.

## Variations

Fewer `panels` gives wide, slow pleats; 10 or more gives a tight concertina.
`foldAngle` sets how closed the card starts: near 89 it starts as a thin
strip, 30–45 starts half open for a gentler move. `shade={0}` removes the fold
shading for a flat graphic look.
