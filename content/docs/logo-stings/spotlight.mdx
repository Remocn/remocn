## Usage

`Spotlight` puts your logo on a dark stage, dimmed almost to black. A soft
cone of light hangs from above the top edge with a pool on the floor. It
searches the left side, swings past the logo to the right, then springs back
and lands on center. The logo brightens as the beam covers it, and once the
beam settles the house lights come up: a faint ambient glow fills the stage,
the beam eases off, and the logo holds at full brightness.

```tsx
import { Img, staticFile } from "remotion";
import { Spotlight } from "@/components/remocn/spotlight";

export const LogoSting = () => (
  <Spotlight beamColor="#FFFFFF" stageColor="#111111">
    <Img src={staticFile("logo.svg")} style={{ height: 120 }} />
  </Spotlight>
);
```

`children` are centered in the frame at their own size. Lighting is a CSS
`brightness()` filter on the children, so it works on images, SVG and text
alike, and their colors are never tinted by `beamColor`. The beam is drawn in
one SVG with a Gaussian blur and scales with the composition.

## Timing

At 30 fps and `speed={1}`:

| Frames | Motion |
| --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Beam fades on at the left of the stage |
| 0–12 | Beam searches from 14% to 27% of the width |
| 12–26 | Beam swings across, overshooting the logo to 74% |
| 26–49 | Beam springs back, passes center slightly and settles on the logo |
| 38–50 | House lights: ambient glow comes up, beam drops to 65%, the logo reaches full brightness |

The docs preview runs 80 frames. `speed` scales the timeline.
