## Usage

`StringArt` draws a modular times table. `pins` points sit on a ring, and a
thread runs from every pin `i` to pin `k × i`, wrapping around the ring. For
`k = 2` the threads envelope a cardioid, for `k = 3` a nephroid, and for
`k = 4` a three-cusp epicycloid. Pin 0 sits on the left, so the figures are
symmetric about the horizontal axis.

The pins fade in first. Then the threads are strung one pin after another,
each growing from its pin to its target. After a short pause the multiplier
walks from `from` to `to` one whole step at a time: each step eases through
the in-between tables, which morph smoothly, and then holds on the clean
figure. A short band of threads in `accent` rides along the stringing head
and keeps circling the ring after the figure lands. A monospaced label in
the corner shows the running rule, `i → k · i mod pins`.

```tsx
import { StringArt } from "@/components/remocn/string-art";

export const TimesTable = () => <StringArt />;
```

The component fills its parent and paints its own `background`. The ring
radius is 42% of the short side, centered, and strokes scale with the
composition. All geometry comes from the Remotion frame, so renders are
deterministic.

## Timing

At 30 fps, `speed={1}` and the defaults (`from={2}`, `to={4}`):

| Frames | Motion |
| --- | --- |
| 0–12 | The pins fade in |
| 0–60 | Threads are strung one pin after another, easing in and out, into a cardioid |
| 50–66 | The label fades in |
| 60–84 | The cardioid holds |
| 84–168 | The multiplier eases from 2 to 3; the figure morphs into a nephroid |
| 168–204 | The nephroid holds |
| 204–288 | The multiplier eases from 3 to 4 |
| 288 | The three-cusp figure lands and holds |
| 60 onward | The accent band circles the ring at 0.9 pins per frame, a lap every 222 frames |

`stringFrames` sets the stringing time and `morphFrames` the whole morph.
The morph starts 24 frames after the stringing ends and is divided into one
equal share per whole step between `from` and `to`. Each share spends 70%
easing and 30% holding. The figure stops changing at
`stringFrames + 24 + morphFrames − 0.3 × share` frames, 288 at the defaults,
and the docs preview adds a short hold at 340. Divide by `speed` for faster
playback. Only the accent band keeps moving after that; set `sweep={0}` for
a fully still end frame.

If `to − from` is not a whole number, the holds land on equal fractions of
the span rather than on whole multipliers.

## Variations

`from={1}` starts from the empty table and grows the cardioid out of
nothing. Higher multipliers give more cusps: `to={6}` ends on a five-cusp
figure. Fewer `pins` show individual threads; more give a solid envelope.
`showLabel={false}` drops the corner label.

```tsx
<StringArt
  pins={300}
  from={3}
  to={6}
  morphFrames={360}
  threadOpacity={0.3}
  showLabel={false}
  background="#0b1020"
  color="#c9d4ff"
  accent="#ffd166"
/>
```
