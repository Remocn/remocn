## Usage

`FlowField` draws particles that follow a vector field across the frame. The
field angle at each point is a sum of four seeded sine waves, and it shifts
slowly over the loop. Each particle is born at a seeded position, sometimes
just outside the frame, and steps along the field for its life. Its
head moves first and the trail grows behind it; then the head stops and the
tail catches up, so trails grow in and drain out instead of popping. Births
are spread over the life, so the frame always holds a steady population.

Each trail is split into six age segments that get fainter and thinner
toward the tail. A seeded share of particles, `accentRatio`, is drawn in
`accent`.

```tsx
import { FlowField } from "@/components/remocn/flow-field";

export const Texture = () => <FlowField />;
```

The component fills its parent and paints its own `background`. Particle
speed and stroke width scale with the short side of the composition, and
`strokeWidth` is in px at 720p. Positions come from Remotion's seeded
`random()`, so a `seed` always produces the same field and every frame
renders the same way.

## Timing

The field and every particle's life cycle repeat every `loopFrames / speed`
frames, 300 frames (10 seconds) at the defaults. The last frame of the loop
flows into the first without a seam, so a `Sequence` of any multiple of the
period, or a Remotion `<Loop>` of that length, plays as a continuous
background.

A particle lives `loopFrames / round(loopFrames / lifeFrames)` frames, 150
at the defaults, so a whole number of lives fits in one loop. The trail is
capped at 60% of the life: the head travels for `life − trail` frames, then
holds while the tail drains.

There is no build-up: frame 0 already shows a full population.

## Performance

Each frame recomputes every visible trail from the particle's birth, so the
cost grows with `particles × lifeFrames`. The defaults render quickly; 800
particles with 300-frame lives is about five times heavier. Lower
`particles` before lowering `trailLength` if renders get slow.

## Variations

A high `trailLength` with a low `particles` count gives long, sparse strands.
`accentRatio={0}` keeps the field monochrome, and `accentRatio={1}` draws
everything in the accent. Change `seed` for a different field layout.

```tsx
<FlowField
  particles={160}
  trailLength={110}
  strokeWidth={2}
  accentRatio={0.25}
  background="#0b1020"
  color="#9fb4ff"
  accent="#ffffff"
  seed={5}
/>
```
