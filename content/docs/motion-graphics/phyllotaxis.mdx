## Usage

`Phyllotaxis` places seeds the way a sunflower head does: seed `i` sits at
`i × 137.508°`, the golden angle, at a radius proportional to `√i`. The
seeds pop in from the center outward, each with a small overshoot, and the
outer ones are drawn a little larger.

When the head is grown, every `arm`-th seed turns `accent` and swells
slightly, and a faint accent line connects them. With a Fibonacci number
these seeds line up into one spiral arm. Then a soft pulse travels outward
along the arm again and again, swelling each seed as it passes. The whole
head turns slowly the entire time.

```tsx
import { Phyllotaxis } from "@/components/remocn/phyllotaxis";

export const Growth = () => <Phyllotaxis />;
```

The component fills its parent and paints its own `background`. The head is
centered and its outer edge sits at 44% of the short side, so it keeps its
framing at any aspect ratio. Everything comes from the Remotion frame, so
renders are deterministic.

## Timing

At 30 fps, `speed={1}` and the defaults:

| Frames | Motion |
| --- | --- |
| 0–70 | Seeds pop in from the center outward; seed `i` starts at `growFrames × √(i / count)` and pops over 10 frames |
| 60–74 | The arm seeds turn accent, swell by 25% and the spine line fades in |
| 70–115 | The first pulse travels from the center to the rim |
| every 45 frames | The next pulse starts |
| 0 onward | The head turns at `rotation` degrees per second |

The square-root schedule moves the reveal front outward at a steady radial
speed, so the dense center does not flash in at once. The build is complete
at `growFrames + 14` frames, 74 at the defaults; after
that the pulse repeats every `pulseFrames` frames for as long as the
`Sequence` runs. The docs preview shows two pulses, 165 frames. Divide by
`speed` for faster playback; `rotation` is in degrees per second and scales
with `speed` as well.

The pulse cycle is seamless on its own. With the default `rotation` the head
keeps turning, so the idle never repeats exactly; set `rotation={0}` for a
loop of `pulseFrames` frames after frame 70.

## Variations

`arm` picks which seeds form the accent spiral. Fibonacci numbers (8, 13, 21,
34, 55) give a clean spiral arm; other values give curved spokes or scattered
dots. Lower `count` and raise `size` for a bold, graphic head with few
seeds.

```tsx
<Phyllotaxis
  count={180}
  size={1.4}
  arm={13}
  rotation={-6}
  background="#f6f1e7"
  color="#3d2c1e"
  accent="#e09f3e"
/>
```
