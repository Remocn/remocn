In Remotion, Cover Ring sets a launch statement over a collage of product photos: the photo tiles flip in around the headline, then collapse into an audio-style bar meter above the line, and a short closing caption lands over green light. Use it for a launch or brand-film beat that goes from a long setup line to a punchline.

## How to build this in Remotion

You don't need `@remotion/media-utils` audio data or a `TransitionSeries` of collage scenes. Install the component and pass your photos and copy:

```bash
npx shadcn@latest add @remocn/cover-ring
```

It is one scene on a schedule from `timeline.ts`. Tiles are `<Img>` elements that flip in with `spring()` and `interpolate()`; the meter is the same tiles reshaped into bars, so it is a visual, not driven by audio. The ground is a live gradients.sh shader from the remocn `gsh` helper.

## Good to know

- Length: 132 frames of motion at 30 fps (264 at 60 fps) with the default props. The demo composition plays 150 frames, which adds a hold. The Usage example below is a 60 fps composition, so the numbers it hands to Remotion are doubled, see [Frame rate](/docs/getting-started/installation#frame-rate).
- Installs: `remotion`, `@remotion/google-fonts` from npm, plus the remocn helpers `gsh`, `glide`, `frame-rate`, `timeline`. The shadcn CLI copies the source into your project.
- Font: loads Geist (the line) and Newsreader italic (the last caption word) itself through `@remotion/google-fonts`. Pass `fontFamily` to use your own face, and load that font in your project.
- Pass `images` with your own image URLs or `staticFile()` paths. The defaults point at files that only exist in the Remocn repo.
- The green comes from the dark theme: the `kiruna` shader preset with accent `#8ff0cc`. Pass `fog` (gradient colors), `ground` (a preset id) or `accent` to change it, or `background` for a solid fill.
- The meter always sits just above the caption; its position is not a prop.

## Usage

```tsx
// src/Root.tsx
import { Composition } from "remotion";
import { CoverRing } from "@/components/remocn/cover-ring";

const CoverRingScene = () => (
  <CoverRing
    images={[staticFile("shots/one.jpg"), staticFile("shots/two.jpg")]}
    line="Launch videos aren't edited anymore"
    caption="They're composed."
  />
);

export const RemotionRoot = () => (
  <Composition
    id="CoverRing"
    component={CoverRingScene}
    durationInFrames={300}
    fps={60}
    width={1280}
    height={720}
  />
);
```

Photo tiles flip in around a setup line, the line blurs away as the tiles collapse into a bar meter, and the caption lands with a pulse. Pass `images` with your own image paths (the defaults only exist in the Remocn repo), plus `line` and `caption`; it ships light and dark through `theme`.

The ground is a live gradients.sh shader (`GshGround`), installed with the component as `@remocn/gsh`. Pick another look with the `ground` preset prop.

## Common questions

### How do I build a launch line over product photos with a bar meter that collapses into a closing caption?

Install Cover Ring with `npx shadcn@latest add @remocn/cover-ring`, pass `images`, `line` and `caption`. The photos collapse into the bar meter and the caption lands under it.

### Is the bar meter driven by audio?

No. The bars are the photo tiles reshaped and animated on the timeline; there is no audio input.

### How do I get the green light?

It is the default dark theme. Change it with `fog`, `ground` or `accent`.
