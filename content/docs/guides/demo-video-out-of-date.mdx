_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

A demo video goes out of date because it is a finished file: once rendered, a renamed button or a
new price means re-recording or paying the editor again. The fix is to keep the video as source code
next to your product, so the label in the video is a string you can change and the MP4 is something
you re-render in a minute. Remocn gives your coding agent the building blocks to write the video that
way, with Remotion, and it lives in git like the rest of your code.

## Why your video keeps going stale

- **A renamed feature** means the video now says something your product doesn't.
- **A redesign** makes every frame show the old UI. A landing-page video showing a two-year-old
  interface quietly tells buyers the product isn't moving.
- **A price change** turns the pricing beat into a liability.
- **The person who made it is gone,** and so is the project file.

Re-recording means redoing the takes, the zooms and the cuts. Hiring means a new brief and a new
invoice. So the video stays wrong.

## The fix: the video is a file in your repo

<ComponentPreview name="table-filter" />

[Table Filter](/docs/product-scenes/table-filter) is a CRM table that filters by a stage, sorts,
selects rows and slides up a bulk-action bar. Every label, row and color is a prop. When your product
renames "Pipeline" to "Deals", the change in the video is the same change:

```diff
- <TableFilter stage="Pipeline" … />
+ <TableFilter stage="Deals" … />
```

Then render again:

```bash
npx remotion render <slug> out/<slug>.mp4
```

Because the video is ordinary React and TypeScript in your project, it goes through code review, and
`git log` shows when the video last matched the product.

## Ask the agent to keep it current

When the UI changes, point your agent at the diff and the video:

<GuidePrompt>

```text
Our UI changed in <PR OR COMMIT>. Update the demo video in src/videos/<slug>
so its labels, prices and screens match the product again. Read the changed
components for the new copy. Don't change the timing or the storyboard,
only what's on screen. Then render it.
```

</GuidePrompt>

To build the first version, follow [Product demo](/docs/guides/product-demo). It reads your repo, so
the video starts in sync.

## When this isn't the fix

- **The video is camera footage** of you or the product in use. Footage can't be re-rendered; plan a
  short re-shoot instead.
- **You need the exact pixels of your running app.** Remocn rebuilds screens as scripted components.
  If you must show the live app, use a tool that re-records it automatically.

<Faq>

**Can a CI job re-render the video?**
Yes. `npx remotion render` runs headless, so you can render on a release tag and upload the MP4. Large
renders can also go to [Remotion Lambda](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/lambda).

**Do I need the same video in vertical and square too?**
Write it once and render each format from the same code: see
[One video, three formats](/docs/guides/one-video-three-formats).

</Faq>
