The recipes default to remocn's look — a warm dark background and one lime accent. Your videos should look like your product instead. Do it once: write a brand brief, have your agent save it in the project, and reference it forever.

<GuideBrandFrames />

## The brand brief

Fill this in and paste it to your agent:

<GuideBrandBrief>

<GuidePrompt copyLabel="Copy" highlight={false} wrap>

```text
Save this as BRAND.md in the project and use it for every video we make here:

# Brand brief

- Product: <NAME> — <one line on what it does>
- Background: <HEX> (dark or light)
- Text color: <HEX>
- Accent color: <HEX> — use sparingly, one accented element per beat at most
- Font mood: <e.g. clean and geometric / warm and rounded / mono and technical>
- Logo: <FILE NAME in this folder, or — use the wordmark "NAME">
- Copy tone: <e.g. plain and confident, no exclamation marks>
```

</GuidePrompt>

</GuideBrandBrief>

<GuideBrandLine>

From then on, start every new video prompt with one line: **"Use the brand brief."**

</GuideBrandLine>

## Picking the values

<GuideDefinitions>

- **Background** — Your app's or landing page's background is usually right. Dark backgrounds forgive more.
- **One accent, used sparingly** — If everything is accented, nothing is.
- **Text color** — Off-white on dark, near-black on light. Pure white on pure black reads harsh on video.
- **Font mood** — You don't have to name a font; describe the mood and your agent picks one. If you know your font's name, name it.
- **Logo** — An SVG beats a PNG: it scales cleanly and can be drawn on.

</GuideDefinitions>

## Staying on brand

- Open every new video prompt with "Use the brand brief."
- Rebranded? Ask your agent to update `BRAND.md` — every video after that follows.

<GuideConsistency>

Consistency compounds: three videos with one look read as a channel; three looks read as three experiments.

</GuideConsistency>
