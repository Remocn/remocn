_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

You can make a product demo without a face or a voiceover by letting the words on screen do the
talking: short lines of animated text set up each step, and a scripted product scene shows it
happening. Most viewers watch muted anyway, on X, LinkedIn and Product Hunt's gallery, so a demo
built this way often works better than a narrated one. Remocn has both halves as components (kinetic
type and app scenes), and a coding agent puts them together with your words.

## Why "just narrate it" doesn't work for everyone

- **You don't want to be on camera,** or your accent, your mic or your room isn't how you want the
  product to sound.
- **Feeds autoplay muted.** A demo that depends on narration says nothing until someone taps.
- **Narration ties you down.** Change one feature and the voiceover is wrong too.
- **AI avatars** solve the face problem but add a stranger to your brand.

## The fix: text that speaks, a screen that shows

The Remocn 3 release film runs about three minutes with nobody on camera. Every point is made by
words on screen, numbers that count up and product scenes that act it out, so it reads with the
sound off. Every scene in it is a Remocn component.

Lead each beat with one short line, set big enough to read in two seconds:

<ComponentPreview name="zoom-words" />

[Zoom Words](/docs/typography/zoom-words) reveals a phrase word by word while the camera rides the
line, so a sentence reads like it's being said. Then show the step:

<ComponentPreview name="shot-answers" />

In [Shot Answers](/docs/media/shot-answers) a question rewrites itself word by word into its answer
while a screenshot slides in beside it: the question a narrator would ask, answered by the product.

The pattern for a whole video is line, screen, line, screen. [Words on screen](/docs/guides/words-on-screen)
has the rules for writing the lines.

## Do it

Set up Remocn once ([Setup](/docs/guides/setup)), then:

```text
Make a ~40-second product demo for <PRODUCT NAME> with remocn components (remocn.dev).
No voiceover and no presenter: every beat opens with one short line of animated text,
then a scripted app screen shows that step. Lines:
1. "<THE PROBLEM, ONE SENTENCE>"
2. "<STEP ONE>"
3. "<STEP TWO>"
4. "<THE RESULT>"
Close on <URL>. Text large enough to read on a phone, each line held at least two
seconds. 1920×1080, 60fps.
```

## When this isn't the fix

- **Trust is the product** (consulting, coaching, a personal brand). A face does work text can't.
- **The flow takes explaining,** like an onboarding walkthrough for a complex tool. Narration, or a
  written tutorial, does that better than slogans.
