_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

To make a Product Hunt launch video without hiring an editor, ask a coding agent (Claude Code or
Codex) to build a 30–45 second launch video from Remocn's ready-made scenes: a hook, your product,
three features and your URL. It renders an MP4 on your machine. Upload that to YouTube, because
Product Hunt's gallery takes video as a YouTube link (per its
[help center](https://help.producthunt.com/posting/how-to-post-a-product)), and paste the link into
your launch.

## What makes a launch video work on Product Hunt

- **It's watched muted, in a gallery.** Words on screen carry the story; sound is a bonus.
- **The first two seconds decide.** Open with the problem or the result, not a logo animation.
- **It's short.** 30–45 seconds holds attention; a two-minute tour belongs on your site.
- **It shows the product.** People upvote what they understand, so one clear flow beats five
  features.

## Do it before launch day

1. **Set up once.** In Claude Code, `/plugin install remocn --marketplace Remocn/remocn`
   ([Setup](/docs/guides/setup) has Codex and the rest).
2. **Ask for it** from your product's repo:

   ```text
   /remocn:video 35-second Product Hunt launch video for Tasklane: the problem in one line, the product, three features, the URL
   ```

3. **Direct it** in plain sentences ("hold the URL longer", "make the hook bigger"), then ask it to
   "Render the video."
4. **Upload to YouTube** as Public or Unlisted, check the link opens while signed out, and add it to
   your Product Hunt gallery.

## Also make the square cut

The same video renders square for X and LinkedIn launch posts and vertical for Shorts, from the same

## When this isn't the fix

- **Your launch is a founder story** told to camera. Film yourself; add Remocn titles on top if you
  like.
- **You have no time for any setup today.** A screen recorder with auto-zoom will get you a decent
  video in an hour.

<Faq>

**Can I upload the MP4 directly to Product Hunt?**
Product Hunt's help center asks for a YouTube link for gallery video, so upload the MP4 to YouTube
first.

**Does it need music?**
Not for the gallery, where it plays muted. If you want a track, see
[Music and sound](/docs/guides/music-and-sound).

</Faq>
