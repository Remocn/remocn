_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

Word-by-word captions are made in two steps: a speech-to-text model transcribes the voiceover with
the start and end time of every word, then the captions are rendered so each word appears or lights
up at its own timestamp. Apps like CapCut do both inside their editor. If you build videos in code,
or let a coding agent build them, you can do it for free: transcribe locally with whisper.cpp, then
drop in a Remocn caption component that reads the timed words.

## Two ways to get them

- **In an editing app** (CapCut, Premiere, Descript): auto-captions, pick a preset, export. Best if
  you already edit your videos there.
- **In code** (Remotion + Remocn): the captions are a component in your video project. Best if you
  make many videos from templates, want your own caption style everywhere, or an agent builds your
  videos.

This page is about the second.

## The fix: timed words in, animated captions out

<ComponentPreview name="caption-word-pop" />

[Word Pop](/docs/captions/components/caption-word-pop) puts one big word on screen at a time, each
popping in on a spring the moment it's spoken: the punchy short-form style.

<ComponentPreview name="caption-karaoke" />

[Karaoke](/docs/captions/components/caption-karaoke) keeps the whole phrase on screen and lights up
each word as it's spoken, so viewers can read ahead. That's better for explainers and talking heads.

Both read the same input: an array of words with times from `@remotion/captions`.

```tsx
import { CaptionWordPop } from "@/components/remocn/caption-word-pop";
import captions from "./captions.json";

<CaptionWordPop captions={captions} />
```

## Do it

1. **Transcribe once.** Locally and free with whisper.cpp, or with the OpenAI or ElevenLabs APIs.
   [Get a transcript](/docs/captions/getting-started/get-a-transcript) has each recipe, all ending in
   the same `captions.json`.
2. **Install a style:** `npx shadcn@latest add @remocn/caption-word-pop`.
   [Captions](/docs/captions/getting-started/introduction) lists every style.
3. **Place it** over your footage. [Positioning](/docs/captions/getting-started/positioning) covers
   safe areas for vertical video.

Or hand it to your agent:

<GuidePrompt>

```text
Add word-by-word captions to the talking-head video in public/<FILE>.mp4.
Transcribe it with whisper.cpp to captions.json, then overlay remocn's
caption-word-pop (remocn.dev), centered, inside the vertical safe area.
Render a 1080×1920 MP4.
```

</GuidePrompt>

## When this isn't the fix

- **You edit in CapCut already** and post one video a week. Its auto-captions are faster.
- **You need captions in a language your transcriber handles badly.** Check the transcript before
  styling it; the animation can't fix a wrong word.

<Faq>

**Is it free?**
whisper.cpp runs locally for free and Remocn is MIT-licensed. Remotion is free for individuals and
small companies; see [its license](https://www.remotion.dev/license).

**Can I highlight keywords in a different color?**
Yes. The components take styling props, and since the source is copied into your project you can
change anything, such as coloring words from a list.

</Faq>
