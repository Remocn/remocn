_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

To turn app screenshots into a video that doesn't look like a slideshow, give each screenshot motion
that matches how the app is used: a phone that scrolls a tall screenshot, or a camera that pushes in
on one region at a time with a label. Remocn has both as components that take your image as a prop,
and a coding agent can build the whole video around them and render it to MP4.

## Why a slideshow falls flat

- **Fade, fade, fade** between static images reads as "we didn't have a video".
- **Nothing tells the eye where to look.** A full screenshot at video size is a wall of UI.
- **A desktop screenshot is unreadable in a phone feed** unless the camera moves in.
- **Ken Burns pans** add motion but no meaning.

## The fix: give the screenshot a job

For a mobile app, put a tall screenshot inside a phone and let it scroll:

<ComponentPreview name="phone-scroll" />

[Phone Scroll](/docs/product-scenes/phone-scroll) raises a phone beside a headline and flicks the
screen to each scroll stop with momentum and a touch dot. Pass your screenshot as `src`:

```tsx
<PhoneScroll
  src="/screens/feed-tall.png"
  stops={[930, 1840]}
  label="Find your next stay in"
  labelAccent="two taps."
/>
```

For a web app, tour one screenshot instead of showing many:

<ComponentPreview name="screenshot-tour" />

[Screenshot Tour](/docs/media/screenshot-tour) pushes in on each region you list, dims the rest and
labels the stop, so one screenshot carries three points.

## Do it

Put your screenshots in the Remotion project's `public/` folder (export at 2× for sharp close-ups),
set up Remocn once ([Setup](/docs/guides/setup)) and ask:

<GuidePrompt>

```text
Make a ~30-second video for <PRODUCT NAME> from the screenshots in public/screens/
with remocn components (remocn.dev). Use phone-scroll for the mobile screenshots
and screenshot-tour for the desktop ones. For each, pick the one region that
matters and label it in a few words: <POINT 1>, <POINT 2>, <POINT 3>.
Open with "<HOOK LINE>", close on <URL>. 1920×1080, 60fps.
```

</GuidePrompt>

## Going further

When screenshots aren't enough to show a flow (a click, a result appearing), the agent can rebuild
the screen as a scripted scene: see
[Make a screen recording not look like one](/docs/guides/screen-recording-looks-amateur).

## When this isn't the fix

- **An App Store preview** must show footage captured from the app itself under Apple's guidelines.
  Check their current rules before using rebuilt or animated screens there.
- **You only need a GIF for a README.** A short screen capture is quicker.
