Every video in these guides — and every video you'll invent yourself — is built the same way: as a list of beats.

## Beats

A beat is one idea on screen. A line of text landing. A feature name over a background. A logo drawing itself on. When the idea changes, the beat changes. Three rules cover most of it:

<BuiltRules art={["one-idea", "two-seconds", "beat-count"]}>

- **One idea per beat** — If describing a beat needs the word "and", it's two beats.
- **~2 seconds** — A text beat lives about two seconds — long enough to read once, not twice.
- **5 to 10 beats** — A video is five to ten beats. The [launch video](/docs/guides/launch-video) is eight.

</BuiltRules>

## The skeleton

Every video that works has the same three acts:

<BuiltSkeleton
  acts={[
    { range: "1–2 beats", beats: 2 },
    { range: "3–5 beats", beats: 5 },
    { range: "1 beat", beats: 1 },
  ]}
>

1. **Hook** — Why anyone should care: a pain, a bold claim, a question.
2. **Body** — What it is: features, value, proof.
3. **Close** — What to do next: a command, a URL, a date.

**Not sure what beats to write?** One or two hook beats, three to five body beats, one close beat. That's a video.

</BuiltSkeleton>

## The vocabulary<GuideCount n={8} />

When you describe a beat to your agent, plain words work — and some plain words map especially well to what Remocn can build:

<BuiltVocabulary
  art={["headline", "stack", "background", "cut", "terminal", "app", "logo", "counter"]}
>

- **"an animated headline"** — A line of text that enters with motion
- **"lines that stack up"** — Claims appearing one after another
- **"a living background"** — Slow, animated color behind everything
- **"a hard cut" / "a scene change"** — How one beat hands over to the next
- **"a terminal that types"** — A command line typing itself out
- **"a fake app screen"** — An interface with buttons, menus, and dialogs that act
- **"a logo that draws itself on"**
- **"a counter"** — A number counting up to a milestone

</BuiltVocabulary>

You never need to know what any of these are called internally. Your agent does.

## From beats to prompt

Write your video as a numbered list of beats — one line each, your copy in quotes. That list is 80% of a prompt; add a format line and a style line and it's done:

<GuidePrompt
  copyLabel="Copy"
  marks={["an animated headline", "three lines that stack up", "a terminal that types"]}
>

```text
Build a ~15-second, 1920×1080, 30fps video in this Remotion project,
using remocn components:

1. Hook — an animated headline: "<YOUR LINE>".
2. Body — three lines that stack up: <YOUR CLAIMS>.
3. Close — a terminal that types: `<YOUR CTA>`.

Style: dark background, one accent color <HEX>, calm pacing.
```

</GuidePrompt>
