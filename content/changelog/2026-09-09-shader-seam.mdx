### New components

[Shader Seam](/docs/transitions/shader-seam) reveals the original @remocn
OpenShaders material, then dissolves it in place to uncover the next scene.
The scene exchange happens under the fully opaque shader. Its dark areas fade
first and luminous folds remain briefly, with no directional travel or cropped
edges. Adjust dissolve softness, organic detail and shader time, or supply
another compatible local OpenShaders export.
