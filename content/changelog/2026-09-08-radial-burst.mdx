### New components

[Radial Burst](/docs/effects/radial-burst) is an abstract opening for product
introductions. A circle blooms into sculptural ribbons, twists through a
tilted plane, and folds into a ring that sweeps outward with contour echoes.
Customize the intensity, ribbon curvature, echoes, segment count, palette,
and timing, or turn intensity to zero for a classic geometric burst.
