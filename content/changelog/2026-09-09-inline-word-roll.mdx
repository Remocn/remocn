### New components

[Inline Word Roll](/docs/typography/inline-word-roll) cycles through words beside
a fixed prefix. Each replacement rolls up from below while the line smoothly
recenters around its new width. Configure the words, prefix, suffix, interval
and acceleration; the final word stays visible. Text measurement uses composition
coordinates so scaled website previews retain the correct spacing and clipping.
