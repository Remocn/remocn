### New components

[Shader Text Reveal](/docs/typography/shader-text-reveal) reveals words through
the original @remocn OpenShaders hero field and halftone texture, then settles into clean white type. A dark diagonal
wave clears each word before the next one appears. The shader controls the
letters' surface and subtle edge refraction, with frame-driven playback and
automatic text fitting.
