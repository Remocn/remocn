### New components

[Search Reveal](/docs/ui-blocks/search-reveal) opens with overlapping construction
circles, stretches a dash into a search field, and types your product name.
An optional abstract panel unfolds behind it in remocn purple. Customize the
copy, typing pace, field size, guides, and palette through the preview controls.
