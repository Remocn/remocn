title: Studio staging and operator camera moves
date: 2026-08-21
---

Product demos can now stage and direct one continuous surface with a single component. Stage gives screenshots, recordings, long pages, and live React scenes a perspective studio treatment, then moves an integrated operator camera across them with normalized timeline targets.

<ChangelogPreview name="stage" />

### New component

- [Stage](/docs/layout/stage) — places any surface on a stronger perspective plane and directs the camera with normalized `x`/`y` targets, per-arrival easing, holds, endpoint clamps, zoom, roll, and deterministic handheld shake. Explicit `contentSize` lets the camera travel from top to bottom of a page much taller than the video frame.
