This release adds 491 new pieces for launch videos. Each one covers a single beat: a headline, a number, a product screen, a logo sting or the cut between two scenes. Three new docs sections hold the pieces that had no home before: [Numbers & Stats](/docs/numbers), [Product Scenes](/docs/product-scenes) and [Media & Collage](/docs/media). The rest join Typography, Logo Stings, Transitions, UI Blocks and Social under new sidebar groups. Most pieces ship dark and light. Many sit on an animated gradient ground from the new `gsh` lib, which renders with `--gl=angle`. Copy, data and images are all props.

### New components

**Numbers & Stats.** A new category of 124 pieces where a number is the hero: stat reveals, counters, charts, comparisons, countdowns and prices.

- [Zero Stretch](/docs/numbers/zero-stretch): the zero in a number stretches into a glossy pill that holds a label word.
- [Sankey Flow](/docs/numbers/sankey-flow): flows from several sources merge into one target block.
- [Countdown](/docs/numbers/countdown): a monumental countdown numeral inside a ring resolves into a Live lockup.
- [Price Page](/docs/numbers/price-page): fine-print pricing lines drop away until a huge price remains.

<ChangelogPreview name="zero-stretch" />

**Product Scenes.** A new category of 60 fully staged app screens, each with a beginning, a middle and a payoff. The groups cover web apps, commerce and payments, mobile, analytics, AI and agents, collaboration, dev tools and onboarding.

- [AI Answer Sources](/docs/product-scenes/ai-answer-sources): an AI answer streams in with inline citations, then a source panel opens.
- [Cart Add](/docs/product-scenes/cart-add): a shop page picks a color and size, then a cart drawer slides in.
- [PR Merge](/docs/product-scenes/pr-merge): checks pass one by one and a cursor merges the pull request.
- [Money Send](/docs/product-scenes/money-send): a phone payment screen confirms the transfer with a receipt.

**Media & Collage.** A new category of 10 pieces that put your own screenshots, photos and footage first.

- [Shards to Grid](/docs/media/shards-to-grid): tilted image shards drift in 3D and snap into a media grid.
- [Screenshot Tour](/docs/media/screenshot-tour): the camera pushes into regions of a screenshot and labels each stop.
- [Before After](/docs/media/before-after): a divider sweeps across a framed image to reveal the after version.

**Typography.** 185 pieces in new groups: Statements, Headline reveals, Kinetic posters, Word swaps, Letter mechanics and Quotes.

- [Word Gap Media](/docs/typography/word-gap-media): a video tile slots into the gap between two words.
- [Macro Letter Pan](/docs/typography/macro-letter-pan): a macro camera hops across the letters of a key word, then pulls back to the phrase.

<ChangelogPreview name="word-gap-media" />

**Logo stings, transitions, UI blocks and social.**

- **Logo stings:** 37 name reveals, logo builds and end stings. [Noise Forge Sting](/docs/logo-stings/noise-forge-sting) forges the wordmark out of hot noise. [Flood Sting](/docs/logo-stings/flood-sting) floods the frame with color before the logo lands.
- **Transitions:** 27 wipes, light cuts, type transitions, match cuts and morphs. [Light Burn to Line](/docs/transitions/light-burn-to-line) burns a scene down to a line of light. [Letter Gate](/docs/transitions/letter-gate) opens the gap between two giant letters onto the next scene.
- **UI blocks:** 21 cards, prompt boxes, controls and callouts. [Caret Dolly](/docs/ui-blocks/caret-dolly) dollies along the caret of a prompt as it types.
- **Social:** 13 creator formats, hooks and social proof. [Reels Feed](/docs/social/reels-feed) runs a vertical feed through a story, a poll and a reel.

### Improvements

- The `remocn` agent skill maps every docs section and launch-video beat. It also covers the conventions the new pieces share: `theme`, gradient grounds and passing your own images.
