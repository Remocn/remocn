Thirty-eight new pieces of motion design, the kind a motion designer builds in After Effects for a single beat. They include graphic cuts between scenes and logo stings for an opener or an end card. Generative loops fill a section break. Camera moves and physics give a product shot some weight, and graphic looks restyle a subject as print, film or a technical drawing. Every piece is deterministic, takes your colors and plays from the Remotion frame, so it seeks and renders the same every time.

### New components

**Transitions.** Six graphic cuts for `TransitionSeries`.

- [Iris Wipe](/docs/transitions/iris-wipe) closes a cartoon iris on the subject, holds it as a dot for a beat, then opens on the next scene.
- [Venetian Blinds](/docs/transitions/venetian-blinds) turns the outgoing scene edge-on in staggered slats to reveal the next one.
- [Paper Tear](/docs/transitions/paper-tear) rips the frame down the middle and flings both halves apart with a shake, a seam flash and debris.
- [Pixel Sort](/docs/transitions/pixel-sort) melts the scene into glowing vertical streaks and drops the next one in column by column.
- [Light Leak](/docs/transitions/light-leak) drifts a warm analog leak across the frame and cuts to the next scene under its blow-out.
- [Hyperspace](/docs/transitions/hyperspace) stretches a starfield into radial streaks, tunnels forward and lands on the next scene through a white flash.

**Logo stings.** A new [Logo Stings](/docs/logo-stings) category. Most of them wrap your own logo.

- [Orbit Lock](/docs/logo-stings/orbit-lock): moons spiral down shrinking orbits into a core, which bursts into a ring flash as your logo springs into place.
- [Puzzle Snap](/docs/logo-stings/puzzle-snap): geometric pieces spin in and snap into a mark with spark bursts, then the wordmark slides out.
- [Spotlight](/docs/logo-stings/spotlight): a stage light searches the dark, overshoots and lands on your logo, then the house lights come up.
- [Neon Sign](/docs/logo-stings/neon-sign): any SVG path lights up as glass tubes that stutter on, buzz off once and hold lit.
- [Inflate](/docs/logo-stings/inflate): a flat sliver puffs up like a balloon toy into a glossy mark.
- [Liquid Fill](/docs/logo-stings/liquid-fill): a drop falls into an outlined mark, which fills with sloshing liquid until it turns solid.
- [Accordion Unfold](/docs/logo-stings/accordion-unfold): a folded paper zigzag opens into a flat card with your logo printed across it.
- [Anamorphic Flare](/docs/logo-stings/anamorphic-flare): a point of light stretches into a cinematic lens streak that sweeps the frame and reveals your logo.

**Generative loops.** A new section in [Motion Graphics](/docs/motion-graphics). Most of these loop seamlessly as backgrounds.

- [Pendulum Wave](/docs/motion-graphics/pendulum-wave): a row of tuned pendulums drifts from a line into waves and braids, then realigns.
- [Flow Field](/docs/motion-graphics/flow-field): hundreds of particles ride a seeded vector field and leave fine fading trails.
- [Isometric Build](/docs/motion-graphics/isometric-build): an isometric grid springs up row by row into a stepped block sculpture with one accent tower.
- [Life Grid](/docs/motion-graphics/life-grid): Conway's Game of Life on a seeded grid, with newborn cells flashing in the accent.
- [String Art](/docs/motion-graphics/string-art): thread strings a ring of pins as a times table, and the figure morphs from a cardioid into a nephroid and beyond.
- [Topo Lines](/docs/motion-graphics/topo-lines): contour lines around seeded peaks breathe and drift, with one line picked out in the accent.
- [Phyllotaxis](/docs/motion-graphics/phyllotaxis): seeds spiral out at the golden angle into a sunflower head, then a Fibonacci arm pulses outward.

**Camera and physics.** A new [Camera & Physics](/docs/camera) category.

- [Parallax Depth](/docs/camera/parallax-depth): a layered landscape trucks sideways, with near layers sliding faster than far ones. You can pass your own layers.
- [Dolly Zoom](/docs/camera/dolly-zoom): the vertigo shot. A corridor stretches while the subject keeps its exact size.
- [Turntable](/docs/camera/turntable): a product card with real thickness spins through its front, edge and back in CSS 3D.
- [Tiny Planet](/docs/camera/tiny-planet): a flat skyline bends into a tiny planet, then keeps turning beside a sun.
- [Exploded View](/docs/camera/exploded-view): a stack of isometric slabs springs apart with leader lines and numbered labels, then clicks back together.
- [Gravity Pile](/docs/camera/gravity-pile): shapes or your own items rain down, bounce and settle into a heap in a deterministic simulation.
- [Cloth Reveal](/docs/camera/cloth-reveal): a satin sheet is yanked off your content from one corner and flutters out of frame.
- [Impact Shake](/docs/camera/impact-shake): heavy content slams down with a squash, a camera jolt, an RGB split and a dust kick.

**Graphic looks.** A new [Graphic Looks](/docs/graphic-looks) category.

- [Riso Print](/docs/graphic-looks/riso-print): a subject printed in two risograph inks, with the accent plate landing off-register on grainy paper.
- [Line Boil](/docs/graphic-looks/line-boil): an SVG outline is drawn by hand, boils on threes like cel animation and fills with color.
- [Blueprint](/docs/graphic-looks/blueprint): a product wipes into a technical drawing with a grid, linework, dimension lines and a callout.
- [Duotone Beat](/docs/graphic-looks/duotone-beat): a portrait as a two-ink halftone that swaps its ink pair and punches in on every beat.
- [Comic Panels](/docs/graphic-looks/comic-panels): the frame cracks into a three-panel comic page, and the action panel bursts into a full-frame POW.
- [Long Shadow](/docs/graphic-looks/long-shadow): a flat icon pops in casting a long shadow, then the light swings and the shadow sweeps around.
- [Synthwave](/docs/graphic-looks/synthwave): a looping retro backdrop with a scrolling perspective grid, a striped sun and mountain ranges.
- [Brick Mosaic](/docs/graphic-looks/brick-mosaic): toy bricks drop row by row onto a baseplate and build any pixel-art image.
- [X-Ray Scan](/docs/graphic-looks/x-ray-scan): a scanner bar sweeps across a device and reveals its internals as white linework.
