date: 2026-09-01

Templates are complete Remotion videos that can be installed and used as-is. They now have a dedicated registry namespace and documentation tab.

This template was retired from the catalog on September 11, 2026.

### New components

- Introducing Product is the first installable template: a complete 53-second product story with kinetic positioning, editable product UI, benefit scenes, and an orbital brand outro. Its Remotion Studio schema exposes content, colors, assets, every scene duration, layout values, and easing presets.
