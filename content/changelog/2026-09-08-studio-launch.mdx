### New

[Studio Launch](/docs/templates/studio-launch) is a 35.5-second launch template
with remocn branding, editable product interfaces, landscape scenes, and camera
transitions. It includes phone and desktop demonstrations, a course editor,
gated content, and a night-to-day reveal.

Change the brand, copy, images, and soundtrack, or edit any scene in code.
