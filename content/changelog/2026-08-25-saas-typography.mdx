### New components

- [Word Stream](/docs/typography/word-stream) builds narration-paced phrases with blurred previews of the next word.
- [Word Push](/docs/typography/word-push) drives new words into a phrase while easing the line toward its new center.
- [Sheen Slide In](/docs/typography/sheen-slide-in) settles a title from the right while a restrained highlight crosses the glyphs.
- [Squeeze In](/docs/typography/squeeze-in) reveals a word before compressing its tracking in one coordinated move.
- [Fog Rise](/docs/typography/fog-rise) stands text upright through perspective, blur, and a center-out reveal.
- [Caret Swap](/docs/typography/caret-swap) consumes one phrase with a caret block and types the replacement.
- [Zoom Words](/docs/typography/zoom-words) tracks a growing line at close range while the shot pulls into focus.
- [Centered Word Build](/docs/typography/centered-word-build) snaps words into view from below while the centered phrase pushes closer on every beat, then wipes away from left to right.
