A new Motion Graphics category: flat geometry built the way motion designers build shape layers. Three openers borrow from Bauhaus, De Stijl and Truchet tiles, and three small accents fire on a click, a landing or a beat, as often as a video needs them. Alongside them come three pieces that show a product at work: the keys behind a shortcut, a code change, and an AI agent answering.

### New components

- [Bauhaus Build](/docs/motion-graphics/bauhaus-build) — flat circles, arcs, squares and bars tumble corner over corner on a hidden grid into a frieze, then rebuild around an open band for the title.
- [Mondrian Split](/docs/motion-graphics/mondrian-split) — black lines cut the frame into a seeded De Stijl grid, primaries wipe into the cells, the grid reflows like rubber, and one cell pushes its own lines out to become the next scene.
- [Truchet Flip](/docs/motion-graphics/truchet-flip) — quarter-circle tiles snap a quarter turn as a wave crosses the grid, rerouting the lines until they settle into rings, a calm field or a circle.
- [Trim Burst](/docs/motion-graphics/trim-burst) — short strokes shoot out of a point like Trim Paths, each start chasing its end until the stroke zips shut in flight.
- [Speed Lines](/docs/motion-graphics/speed-lines) — streaks trail a flying element at its real speed and die as it lands with a squash and a small bounce, or radial lines snap onto an impact point.
- [Squiggle](/docs/motion-graphics/squiggle) — a line draws itself along a course as a wave that swims forward and calms, in wave, zigzag, loop or coil shapes.
- [Keystroke](/docs/effects/keystroke) — physical keycaps press a shortcut in real order, the modifiers held while the letter taps, on the same frame as the UI it triggers.
- [Code Morph](/docs/ui-blocks/code-morph) — Magic Move for code: surviving tokens glide to their new line and column while removed code dissolves and new code fades in.
- [Agent Run](/docs/ai/agent-run) — a brand-neutral AI agent run where tool calls resolve against a real-seconds timer and the markdown answer streams in bursts.
