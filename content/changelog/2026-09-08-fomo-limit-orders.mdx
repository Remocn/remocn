### New

[Fomo Limit Orders](/docs/templates/fomo-limit-orders) recreates a complete
18-second trading-product introduction as editable React and SVG source.
It includes six scenes, camera movements, a slider-to-caret morph, price entry,
a ball following the rounded phone border, and an order confirmation.

Customize the copy, brand, colors, and soundtrack. The template includes local
font files and renders without the original reference video.
