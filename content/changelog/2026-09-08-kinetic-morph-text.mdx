### New components

[Kinetic Morph Text](/docs/typography/kinetic-morph-text) turns editable phrases
into a sequence of individual letter flights, rotations, and soft glyph
changes. Shared letters move to their new positions, with contour trails that
clear as each phrase settles. Controls cover the copy, motion, colors, timing,
and looping.
