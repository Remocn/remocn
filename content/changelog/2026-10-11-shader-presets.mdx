36 ready-made looks for the gradient shaders, each one its own registry item. Install `shader-mesh-gradient-dusk` or `shader-warp-silk` and drop it in: the palette and parameters live in the installed file, the base shader comes along as a dependency, and props you pass still override the preset. Every preset has its own docs page, nested under its base shader in the sidebar, and each base shader page shows a gallery of its presets.

<ChangelogPreview name="shader-mesh-gradient-aurora" />

### New components

- Nine presets each for [Mesh Gradient](/docs/shaders/components/shader-mesh-gradient), [Grain Gradient](/docs/shaders/components/shader-grain-gradient), [Warp](/docs/shaders/components/shader-warp) and [Swirl](/docs/shaders/components/shader-swirl), from soft pastel washes like `lavender` and `blush` to loud ones like `acid` and `hypno`.
- On a preset, `speed` scales the preset's own pace instead of replacing it, so `speed={2}` always means twice as fast.
