### New components

[Brand Guidelines](/docs/templates/brand-guidelines) is a complete 17.9-second
identity film for the fictional Form Study studio: an original architectural
mark, sliding color panels, typed specimens, rapid style changes, a layered
object collage, and a three-word closing reveal.

All brand copy, colors, photographs, and the logo are replaceable. New still-life
assets and local font files keep the default install self-contained. Five named
scenes, a Studio schema, and a reduced-motion variant make the template editable
and reusable. No source-brand assets or soundtrack are included.
