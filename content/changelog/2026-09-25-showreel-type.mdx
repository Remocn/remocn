Six typography pieces pulled from a code-built motion showreel: heavy words on flat color fields that snap, chase, echo, repeat, spin and land. Each one isolates a single trick and works with any text.

<ChangelogPreview name="type-wall" />

### New components

- [Selection Snap](/docs/typography/selection-snap) — a thin word snaps into a design-tool selection while its weight runs from Thin to Black on a variable font, then handles pop in and a size badge counts up.
- [Lead Bar Type](/docs/typography/lead-bar-type) — a cursor bar runs along the baseline, the letters chase it in smeared and land with an overshoot, and the bar settles into an underline clear of the descenders.
- [Echo Stack](/docs/typography/echo-stack) — the word focuses in while outline copies unfold above and below it and keep drifting, looping without a seam.
- [Type Wall](/docs/typography/type-wall) — rows of one word fill the frame in heavy, narrow and outline faces, running in alternating directions at seeded speeds around an accent band.
- [Ring Text](/docs/typography/ring-text) — a phrase runs around a tilted 3D band that spins, readable on the near side and mirrored on the far side, with the band closing to fit any text.
- [Period Drop](/docs/typography/period-drop) — the word rises out of its line, then a colored dot falls, bounces twice with squash and stretch, and settles as the period.
