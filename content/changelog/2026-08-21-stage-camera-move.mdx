---
title: Studio staging and operator camera moves
date: 2026-08-21
---

Product demos can now separate art direction from camera direction. Stage gives any screenshot, recording, or live React scene a single-plane studio treatment; Camera Move directs a sustained shot around it with storyboard-friendly coordinates.

<ChangelogPreview name="stage" />

### New components

- [Stage](/docs/layout/stage) — places any scene on a perspective plane with a backdrop, contact shadow, floor reflection, directional light, and a soft 24-frame settle.
- [Camera Move](/docs/layout/camera-move) — directs one continuous scene with camera-space keyframes, a separate easing for every arrival, endpoint clamps, natural holds, centered zoom, and deterministic handheld shake.
