### New components

[Release Teaser](/docs/templates/release-teaser) is a sixteen-second cinematic
announcement for the fictional Orvio 2. Five editable statements build to a
release lockup over an original dimensional open-ring mark with moving edge light.

The template includes configurable branding, copy, colors, custom logos,
deterministic geometry and camera movement, optional audio, and a reduced-motion
variant. All visual layers are generated from code; no source movie or inherited
brand assets are included.
