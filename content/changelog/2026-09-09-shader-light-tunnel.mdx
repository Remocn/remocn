### New components

[Light Tunnel](/docs/shaders/components/shader-light-tunnel) projects the original
@remocn OpenShaders hero field into a tunnel with straight forward travel.
The original colors, silk folds and halftone remain intact. The folds rotate
around a fixed central vanishing point, making the circular motion visible
throughout the tunnel. Three repetitions of the hero ribbons fill the tunnel
with more spiral lines. Controls adjust flight speed, spiral motion, spiral
count, depth and exposure. The hero's material phase stays fixed so its color
cycle does not dim the ribbons during motion. Light folds stream inward toward
the center on top of the circular motion, with a fast inward flow inspired by
the reference clip's 1–3 second tunnel sequence.

The center blends into a soft blur, removing the outlined dark circle at the
vanishing point while keeping the outer ribbons sharp.
