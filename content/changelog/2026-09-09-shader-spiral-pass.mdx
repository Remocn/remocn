### New components

[Shader Spiral Pass](/docs/transitions/shader-spiral-pass) accelerates straight
into a luminous spiral and reveals the next scene through its soft center.
It reuses the Light Tunnel shader with its fixed color phase, rotating ribbons
and fast inward flow. Controls adjust camera push, exit softness and shader motion.
The incoming scene stays clipped inside a narrow, softly edged exit until the
final approach, keeping its content from showing through the spiral walls.
